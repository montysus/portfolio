Principles

No hype. No theater. Engineered work, honestly presented. Every system here has hit dirt, water, or air. Prototypes that never leave the CAD file don't count.

What I do

Full stack autonomy,
hardware to flight code.

DESIGN

Mechatronic design

CAD, FEA, DFM. Payload mechanisms, frames, actuation, designed to be manufacturable, not just printable.

CONTROL

Flight & motion control

PID tuning, state estimation, sensor fusion. ROS & PX4 stacks. Real time guarantees on embedded targets.

AUTONOMY

Perception & planning

LiDAR/EO fusion, A* & RRT planners, obstacle avoidance. On device inference. No cloud round trip.

About

Antonio Montero.

Spanish robotics engineer, professional drone pilot. Moved to Norway to work on autonomous systems in one of the most demanding operating environments in the world.

Degree in Robotics and Control Systems. University was the theory; the real education came from personal projects, failed prototypes, and field iterations.

I don't sell vision decks. I ship systems, and I want to work with people who are building real things at the edge of what's possible.
